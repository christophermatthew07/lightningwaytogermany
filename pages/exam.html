Exam Strategy that Passes

The Goethe B1 exam is not about German fluency — it’s about execution.

📖 Lesen — How to Score Fast

  • ✔ Do the parts in this order: 3 → 4 → 5 → 1 → 2
  • ✔ Eliminate answers that are not logical, not “wrong German”
  • ✔ Underline keywords (names, numbers, time, opinion)
  • ✔ If a sentence repeats the text 100%, it’s often a trap

Golden rule:
Lesen tests logic and matching — not translation.

🎧 Hören — Meaning Over Words

  • ✔ Listen for verbs, not adjectives
  • ✔ Underline keywords before audio starts
  • ✔ Focus on the meaning of the sentence, not exact wording
  • ✔ First answer is often wrong — wait for confirmation

If two answers sound similar, choose the one that matches intention, not vocabulary.

✍️ Schreiben — Time & Control

  • ✔ Plan first (5 minutes), write later
  • ✔ Follow the given points strictly
  • ✔ B1 sweet spot: 80–100 words
  • ✔ Simple sentences > risky grammar

Examiners reward structure, not creativity.

🗣️ Speaking — Stay Calm, Stay Clear

  • ✔ Memorize structure, not sentences
  • ✔ Pause = control, not weakness
  • ✔ Speak slower than normal
  • ✔ If stuck, rephrase simply
